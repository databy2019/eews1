LITERATURE BENCHMARKS & INTERPRETATION
Western Java scenario: a published EEW performance study estimated about 34% casualty reduction for an M8 subduction scenario and about 3% for an M7 inland scenario; the study also found that adding roughly 3 seconds of lead time materially changed the estimated reduction.

Human response: recent literature indicates that even 5–10 seconds can support simple protective actions, but effectiveness depends strongly on preparedness, alert comprehension and context.

Operational performance: the BMKG-linked western Java pilot reported detection around 6 seconds after origin time for the 2022 Cianjur earthquake in its real-time tests. Alert timeliness, correctness, missed alerts, false alerts and latency are therefore explicit dashboard dimensions.

Important: all numbers above the benchmark notes are illustrative scenario values for dashboard prototyping, not a claim of national Indonesian loss reduction.
